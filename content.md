## Membership (verbatim rates from /membership-rates)
- **Individual** — one adult 18+, full access. **$165/month plus tax.** Enrollment $600.
- **Couple** — one adult 18+ plus one other household member (adult or child).
  **$272/month plus tax.** Enrollment $800.
- **Family** — up to two adults 18+ in the same household plus all dependents under 21
  (under 24 if full-time student). **$339/month plus tax.** Enrollment $1,150.
- All memberships are **month-to-month** — no term commitment.
- "It is our goal to protect the value of our memberships and treat our members with
  respect by ensuring fair and consistent pricing across our membership base."
